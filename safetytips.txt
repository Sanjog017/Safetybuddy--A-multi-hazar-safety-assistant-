[EARTHQUAKE]
==================================================
              EARTHQUAKE SAFETY
==================================================

1. Cover your head and neck
2. Stay away from windows and heavy furniture
3. Drop, Cover, and Hold On
4. Keep emergency supplies ready
5. Stay calm and avoid panic\

[EARTHQUAKE_END]


[FLOOD]
==================================================
                 FLOOD SAFETY
==================================================

1. Move to higher ground immediately
2. Avoid walking or driving through flooded areas
3. Stay updated with flood warnings
4. Disconnect electrical appliances if safe to do so
5. Keep emergency supplies ready

[FLOOD_END]


[FIRE]
==================================================
                  FIRE SAFETY
==================================================

1. Install smoke alarms and test them regularly
2. Practice fire escape plans
3. Keep flammable materials away from heat sources
4. Never leave cooking unattended
5. Keep a fire extinguisher nearby

[FIRE_END]


[LANDSLIDE]
==================================================
               LANDSLIDE SAFETY
==================================================

1. Stay away from steep slopes and unstable ground

2. Watch for cracks in the ground or leaning trees
3. Move to safer areas immediately
4. Listen to local authority warnings
5. Keep emergency essentials ready

[LANDSLIDE_END]


[STORM]
==================================================
                 STORM SAFETY
==================================================

1. Stay indoors and away from windows
2. Avoid using electrical appliances
3. Keep flashlights and batteries ready
4. Do not take shelter under trees
5. Follow weather updates regularly

[STORM_END]


[ROADACCIDENT]
==================================================
            ROAD ACCIDENT SAFETY
==================================================

1. Always wear a seatbelt
2. Follow traffic rules and speed limits
3. Avoid distractions while driving
4. Do not drink and drive
5. Keep a first aid kit in your vehicle

[ROAD_END]


[FIRSTAID]
==================================================
             FIRST AID INSTRUCTIONS
==================================================

1. Clean cuts and scrapes properly
2. Cool burns using cool water
3. Apply clean bandages on wounds
4. Perform Heimlich maneuver during choking
5. Call emergency services if needed

[FIRSTAID_END]


[EMERGENCYCONTACT]
==================================================
             EMERGENCY CONTACTS
==================================================

1. Police: 100 (Nepal)
2. Fire Brigade: 101
3. Ambulance: 102
4. Traffic Police: 103
5. Disaster Emergency: 1149

6. Keep local emergency numbers saved in your phone
7. Share your live location with family during emergencies
8. Know the nearest hospital and police station in your area

[EMERGENCYCONTACT_END]
